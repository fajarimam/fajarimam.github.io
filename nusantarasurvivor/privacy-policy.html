Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download or play Nusantara Survivor: Legenda Mistis (the "Game"), use its online features, or contact the Game's operator ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores information locally to support gameplay and offline functionality. This may include:

  • Game progress, unlocked characters, coins, and upgrades.
  • Equipment, talents, and gameplay preferences.
  • Audio, display, and control settings.
  • Downloaded game assets and cached configuration files.
  • Cached More Games images and promotional information.

Local storage allows the Game to remember your preferences and use previously downloaded content. Storing information locally does not, by itself, mean that it is sent to us.

2. Online Connections and Downloads

An internet connection is required for the initial download of necessary game content. The Game uses Google Play Asset Delivery to obtain game assets and may connect to content servers or hosting providers to retrieve configuration files, advertising settings, promotional information, and images.

Updated settings and content may be cached on your device. When your device connects to an online service, the receiving service processes technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and requested resource. Hosting providers may record this information in server logs.

Offline gameplay may be available after required downloads are complete. Advertising, content updates, external links, and other online features require an internet connection.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements, including interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements.

Depending on the SDK version, enabled features, device settings, and applicable consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Advertising identifiers, app set identifiers, and other applicable device or account identifiers.
  • App and advertising interactions, including launches, taps, advertisement views, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information relating to the Game and advertising SDK.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, provide advertising-related analytics, and detect or prevent fraud and abuse. Personalized advertising depends on applicable settings, eligibility, and consent requirements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. The Game processes advertisement completion or reward events to determine whether to grant the corresponding in-game reward.

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Advertising Technologies

4. Information You Provide When Contacting Us

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, and provide support. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To save preferences and maintain local game progress.
  • To download, update, and cache necessary content.
  • To display advertisements and administer ad rewards.
  • To diagnose technical issues and protect service security.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, in response to valid legal requests, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or legal rights.

7. More Games and External Links
